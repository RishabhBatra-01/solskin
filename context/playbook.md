# SOLSKIN — Marketing Playbook

How to build each asset type. Use these structures; don't reinvent per request.

## The five ad angles (always ship a set, one per angle)
1. **Problem-led** — the itch, the reaction, the patchy ankles. Opens on the pain.
2. **Proof-led** — 250k customers, 97.2%, Trustpilot, dermatologically tested.
3. **Identity-led** — "sensitive skin girlies", "the one who always goes orange".
4. **Mechanism-led** — why it works: aloe + hyaluronic acid + no parabens/sulfates.
5. **Offer-led** — bundle to free delivery (£45), 30-day guarantee, event urgency.

## UGC video script structure (TikTok / Reels / Meta, 20–35s)
```
HOOK (0–3s)      Visual + spoken. Must work on mute — text on screen.
PROBLEM (3–8s)   Their specific pain, in their words.
TURN (8–12s)     "Then I found..." — introduce SOLSKIN, not as an ad.
PROOF (12–20s)   Ingredient/derm/social proof + application demo.
RESULT (20–28s)  The glow. Real skin, real light, no filter language.
CTA (28–35s)     Soft. "Linked below" / "It's SOLSKIN."
```
Always deliver: 3 hook variants per script, shot notes, and on-screen text.

## Hook bank (starting set — expand as winners emerge)
- "If self tan makes you itch, watch this."
- "POV: sensitive skin but you refuse to be pale forever."
- "Three half-used bottles under my sink. Here's the one I finished."
- "I stopped tanning for two years because of my skin."
- "Nobody tells you the reason your tan goes patchy isn't the tan."
- "Dermatologically tested self tan exists and I'm annoyed I didn't know."
- "Rate my tan but I have the world's most reactive skin."

## Static ad structure
- Image: real skin, natural light, no heavy retouching.
- Headline ≤ 7 words, benefit or pain.
- One proof element (badge/stat/star rating).
- Price or offer only in offer-led variants.

## Email
- **Welcome (5)**: story → sensitive-skin proof → how to apply → objections/FAQ → offer
- **Abandoned cart (3)**: reminder → objection-killer (guarantee) → soft urgency
- **Post-purchase (3)**: how to apply well → maintain/extend the tan → cross-sell tools/shimmer
- Subject lines: lowercase, ≤ 42 chars, curiosity or empathy, no ALL CAPS, no "!!!"

## Landing page structure
1. Hero: sensitive-skin promise + product + rating
2. Problem agitation (the three half-used bottles)
3. Mechanism: ingredients, what's left out
4. Proof: reviews, stats, derm testing, Trustpilot
5. How to use (kills the "I'll mess it up" objection)
6. Shade/product picker
7. FAQ = the objection list from `products.md`
8. Guarantee + delivery + CTA

## Offer architecture
- Free delivery at £45 → build bundles that land at £45–55
- 30-day money-back guarantee is the risk reversal in every ad and every LP
- Event urgency (holiday/wedding/weekend) is the seasonal hook, not permanent discounting

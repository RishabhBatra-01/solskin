## Primary avatar (working hypothesis — CONFIRM)
**"Reactive Rosie" — 24–38, UK, sensitive/reactive skin**

- Loves a tan, has been let down by tan. Owns three half-used bottles under the sink.
- Has skin that flares: itchy after mousse, tight after alcohol-heavy formulas, blotchy patches.
- Reads ingredient lists now. Has moved her skincare to "gentle" but her body care hasn't caught up.
- Buys on TikTok/Instagram recommendation more than on brand advertising.
- Trigger moments: holiday booked, event/wedding, first sunny weekend, new season, post-breakup glow-up.

**Pains**
- The reaction: itching, bumps, tightness, redness after tanning
- The failure: patchy ankles, orange palms, streaky knees
- The smell that gives away it's fake
- The guilt of wasting money on yet another bottle
- Feeling washed out / low without a bit of colour

**Desires**
- Look glowy, healthy, "just got back" — not obviously fake
- A product she can use without bracing for a reaction
- A ritual that feels like self-care, not a chore before a night out
- Confidence in her own skin, clothes fitting the way she wants them to feel

**Objections (in her words)**
- "Every tan makes me itch, this'll be the same"
- "I'm rubbish at applying it"
- "£22 for something I might react to"
- "Is it going to look orange on my skin tone?"

**Where she is**
TikTok, Instagram Reels, Pinterest before an event, Trustpilot/Reddit before buying,
beauty group chats.

ACNE · ACTIVE SKIN · ECZEMA · PSORIASIS.
The pop-up quiz segments differently again: Acne-Prone · Dry Skin · Oily Skin · Fine Lines.

rather than from demographics.

followings on site span 1.2k to 1.5m, so the strategy spans nano to mega.

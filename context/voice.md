# SOLSKIN — Voice & Tone

## The voice in one line
Your glowy, straight-talking friend who happens to know the ingredient list.

## Tone dials
| Dial | Where we sit |
|---|---|
| Formal ←→ Casual | 80% casual |
| Serious ←→ Playful | 65% playful |
| Reserved ←→ Bold | 70% bold |
| Clinical ←→ Warm | 85% warm |

## Rules
- **Short sentences.** Then a shorter one.
- Second person. "Your skin", "you'll", not "customers" or "one".
- Lead with the feeling, back it with the fact. *"No itch. No tight skin. Just glow. (That's the aloe and hyaluronic acid doing their job.)"*
- Contractions always. We're, you'll, don't.
- Sparing emoji — one, at the end, never mid-sentence. Never more than one per caption.
- British English. Colour, moisturiser, favourite.
- Confidence without hype. "Actually works" beats "revolutionary".

## Words we use
glow, kind, gentle, streak-free, buildable, ritual, skin-loving, no-drama, sun-kissed,
that lit-from-within thing, your skin's business

## Words we don't
flawless, perfect, fix, correct, flaws, imperfections, "problem areas", miracle, magic,
game-changer, revolutionary, "beach body", "bikini body", anything that implies untanned
skin is a problem to be solved

## Copy patterns that fit us

**The empathy open**
> If tan usually makes your skin itch, you've probably given up on it. Fair.

**The confession**
> I've got three half-used bottles under my sink. This is the only one I've finished.

**The clean contrast**
> Most tans: alcohol, harsh, biscuit smell.
> Ours: aloe, hyaluronic acid, dermatologically tested.

**The proof drop**
> 250,000 people. 97.2% of them didn't go back.

**The soft CTA**
> Give your skin the glow, not the reaction.

## Headline examples (approved feel)
- "Tan that likes your skin back."
- "Sensitive skin? Still allowed a glow."
- "The one you'll actually finish the bottle of."
- "Streak-free. Transfer-free. Drama-free."

**The signature rhythm: the triple negative.** "No streaks, no orange, no drama."
Reuse the structure, vary the contents.
